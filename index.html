02

Scope Boundaries

✓ Explicitly IN Scope

  • Two-pass digit OCR segmentation capable of identifying folded jersey cloth and single vs double digits.
  • Confidence scoring pipeline: auto-approve $\ge 85\%$, flag ambiguous clips ($< 85\%$).
  • 60-second mobile/tablet Coach Micro-Audit UI with high-res jersey zoom and 1-tap reassign/split.
  • Smart Keyframe Selector enforcing front-facing jersey numbers and player faces for thumbnails.
  • Parent 1-tap post-dispatch correction valve ("Wrong kid in this clip?").
  • Push delivery scheduling engine (default: Sunday 9:30 AM local time).

✕ Explicitly OUT of Scope

  • In-app trimming, music mixing, or video editing tools for parents (rejected per Material 2 data).
  • Proprietary WebRTC live broadcast streaming infrastructure.
  • Biometric facial recognition without jersey number verification (privacy & COPPA compliance).
  • Manual coach-only reel assembly (violates SidelineReel's zero-effort promise).
03

Where a Human Stays in the Loop (HITL)

SidelineReel never asks a coach to build a reel by hand. Instead, we use humans strictly as low-friction verifiers where machine confidence is ambiguous.

04

Testable Acceptance Criteria

AC-01 Single vs. Double Digit Fold Disambiguation

Given a match where roster includes both Player A (#4) and Player B (#14),
When OCR detects jersey digit '4' with > 35% fold occlusion on the left flank,
Then system must assign CONFIDENCE < 80% and flag for coach review instead of misrouting to Player #4.

AC-02 Coach 1-Tap Resolution Latency

Given a flagged item with Emma Diaz (#14) and Sophie Clark (#4),
When the coach taps "Confirm as Emma Diaz (#14)",
Then Emma's personal reel must regenerate with the clip in < 3.0 seconds, queue count must decrement by 1, and audit time telemetry must log.

AC-03 Smart Keyframe Thumbnail Scoring

Given an 8-second highlight clip,
When the thumbnail generator extracts keyframe candidates,
Then it must score frames and select one where Jersey_Number_Visibility > 0.90 and Face_Detected == True, explicitly rejecting back-of-jersey or turf slide frames.

AC-04 Delivery Window Guardrail

Given reel rendering completes at 2:15 AM Sunday,
When dispatch scheduler processes notifications,
Then SMS/Push alerts must be held until 9:30 AM in the club’s local time zone unless an explicit coach override is active.

05

Top Two Failure Modes & Detection

Failure Mode 1: Coach Fatigue / Queue Abandonment

The Risk: Busy volunteer coaches fail to open the audit queue before Sunday morning, resulting in stalled reels and angry parents.

What Catches It:
  • Automated SLA Fallback: If unreviewed after 12 hours (or by Sunday 8:00 AM), clips between 70%–84% confidence are auto-assigned with a "Pending Coach Confirmation" badge, ensuring delivery is never blocked.
  • Clips < 70% Confidence: Held in coach archive; team recap delivers on time.
  • Urgency Push: A 1-tap deep link sent Sunday 8:00 AM: "Coach, 2 plays need your 30-second nod."
Failure Mode 2: Swapped Digits & Inversion False Positives (#12 vs #21)

The Risk: In rapid motion (lacrosse, soccer), swapped digits or mirrored jerseys achieve false-high confidence (> 85%) and bypass the coach queue.

What Catches It:
  • Roster Substitution Anomaly Check: Cross-references playing time. If #21 was on the bench during minute 34, clip is flagged regardless of OCR confidence.
  • Real-Time Parent Feedback Circuit Breaker: If > 1 parent on a single team taps "Wrong Kid" within 30 minutes of delivery, further dispatches are automatically paused and flagged for priority review.
